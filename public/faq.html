FAQ

GENERAL

What is SlipRooms?

▼
SlipRooms is a social media app for sports bettors. Create or join chat rooms to "sweat" your bets with others in real-time. Share picks, discuss games, and connect with fellow sports fans.

Is SlipRooms a sportsbook?

▼
NO. SlipRooms is NOT a sportsbook. We do not accept bets, handle money, or facilitate any gambling transactions. We are a social platform for discussing sports and bets.

Is SlipRooms free?

▼
Yes, SlipRooms is completely free to use.

What does "sweating" mean?

▼
"Sweating" is slang for anxiously watching a game when you have a desired outcome. On SlipRooms, you sweat together with others who share the same rooting interest.

ACCOUNT

How old do I have to be to use SlipRooms?

▼
You must be at least 18 years old to use SlipRooms.

Can I change my username/handle?

▼
No, your @handle is permanent and cannot be changed. However, you can change your display name at any time.

How do I delete my account?

▼
Go to Settings and select "Delete Account" or contact support@sliprooms.com.

ROOMS

How do I create a room?

▼
Tap "Create Room" from the Hub, select a sport, pick a game, choose your bet type, and create your room.

How long do rooms last?

▼
Rooms automatically close shortly after the game ends.

Can I be in multiple rooms at once?

▼
Yes, you can join as many rooms as you want.

THE FLOOR (FEED)

What is The Floor?

▼
The Floor is our social feed where you can post updates, share picks, and interact with other users - similar to Twitter.

What is the difference between Friends and Discover?

▼
The Friends tab shows posts from people you follow. The Discover tab shows trending posts from public accounts.

SAFETY & CONTACT

How do I report a user?

▼
Tap on their profile and select "Report User" or contact support@sliprooms.com.

How do I block someone?

▼
Tap on their profile and select "Block User."

How do I contact support?

▼
Email us at support@sliprooms.com
