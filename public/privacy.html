PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: February 2025

1. INTRODUCTION

SlipRooms ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app and website.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account Information:

  • Email address
  • Username and display name
  • Profile picture
  • Password (encrypted)

User Content:

  • Posts, replies, and messages you create
  • Images you upload
  • Rooms you create or join

Usage Data:

  • App activity and interactions
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Analytics data

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Enable social features (posts, messaging, friend connections)
  • Send notifications you have opted into
  • Improve our services
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Respond to support requests

4. INFORMATION SHARING

We do NOT sell your personal information.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers (Firebase/Google for hosting and authentication)
  • Law enforcement if required by law
  • Other users (only content you choose to make public)

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

Your data is stored on secure Firebase/Google Cloud servers. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@sliprooms.com

7. COOKIES AND TRACKING

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze app usage

8. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our app uses third-party services including:

  • Firebase (authentication, database, storage)
  • ESPN API (sports data)
  • Giphy/Tenor (GIF search)

These services have their own privacy policies.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

SlipRooms is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected information from a user under 18, we will delete it.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email.

11. CONTACT US

For privacy questions or concerns:
Email: support@sliprooms.com
